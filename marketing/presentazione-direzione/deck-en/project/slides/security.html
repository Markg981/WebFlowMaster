Security and governance

Built for a company, not for a single developer.

Self-hosted

test data and evidence stay on our servers or in our own S3

Isolated organizations

separation between teams or customers is enforced by the database itself

Enterprise access

SSO with OpenID Connect or SAML, SCIM, roles, scoped API keys

Traceability

append-only audit log; AI stays off until an owner turns it on

A point that matters for the company. We install the platform ourselves: data and evidence don’t leave. Organizations are isolated by the database itself, not just by the application, so several teams or customers can share one installation. Access goes through the corporate single sign-on, with SCIM provisioning, roles and API keys with limited permissions. Every administrative action lands in an append-only audit log. And AI assistance is off until an owner gives explicit consent.
