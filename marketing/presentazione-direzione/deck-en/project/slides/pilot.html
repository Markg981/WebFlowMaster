The pilot

Two weeks, measured against the team’s numbers, with a clear gate.

What we measure

  • time to the first green test
  • fixes needed after recording
  • time to diagnose a failure
  • locators to repair and retries
  • run minutes per flow

What blocks a yes

Even one case of a false green, lost evidence, unauthorized access or a blocked operator reopens the issue and keeps the gate closed until it’s fixed.

You record the final decision, on the report.

The pilot is already structured, with a tool in the product that produces the report. We measure time to the first green test, fixes after recording, diagnosis time, locator maintenance, retries and run minutes, and compare them with what the team measures today on the same flows. What isn’t observed stays unknown; it doesn’t count as zero. And there’s a gate: a false green, lost evidence, unauthorized access or a blocked operator, even once, keeps the yes closed until it’s fixed. You make the decision, on the report.
