A few numbers on where we stand. 823 manual acceptance test cases in 25 areas, run on a full environment with single sign-on, Jenkins and an Android emulator. Over 500 automated test files. Two independent audits, at the end of October and the beginning of November, produced 37 fix batches, all merged. Over 500 pull requests, small and traceable, and today the CI on main is green. The manual is complete in Italian and English. It’s not a prototype: what’s missing is the test with a real team, and that’s the pilot I’m asking for.
