A failure

It arrives with the evidence to understand it.

  • screenshot of the moment
  • video of the run
  • step-by-step trace
  • network capture
  • HTML, PDF, JUnit export
This is where the most time is saved. A failing test carries the screenshot of the moment, the video, the Playwright trace to replay step by step with DOM and network, and the traffic capture. Whoever fixes it doesn’t have to reproduce anything or ask which machine it happened on. The PDF is the evidence to attach to the release or to an audit.
